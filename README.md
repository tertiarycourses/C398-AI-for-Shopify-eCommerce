# AI for Shopify eCommerce

Build, configure and market a live Shopify eCommerce store in one hands-on day — from store setup and products through themes, orders, payments, shipping and marketing.

| Registration | [View course details and register](https://www.tertiarycourses.com.sg/ai-for-shopify-ecommerce.html) |

## About the course

You work on your own Shopify trial store all day. You set up the store and its basic configuration, curate products with variants and SEO metadata, and publish and customise a theme. Then you run the full order lifecycle, configure payments, shipping and taxes for the Singapore market, and launch discounts, analytics, SEO and a newsletter to grow the store. Every lab ends with a "Test it" check, so by the end of the day your store is configured, stocked and measurable.

## Learning outcomes

- **LO1** — Setup Shopify eCommerce store and CMS
- **LO2** — Curate product and web content on Shopify store
- **LO3** — Improve customer experience by customising Shopify theme
- **LO4** — Manage orders on Shopify store
- **LO5** — Manage payment and shipping issues on Shopify store
- **LO6** — Develop and review Shopify store marketing and site performance

## Topics covered

| Topic | Focus | Labs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Getting Started on Shopify | Shopify overview, plans, basic store configuration | Lab 1 |
| 2 · Setting Up Products | Product details, search engine listing, tags, variants | Lab 2 |
| 3 · Customizing Theme | Theme Store, theme editor, theme code, collections, pages | Labs 3–4 |
| 4 · Managing Orders | Order lifecycle, fulfillment, Timeline, refunds and cancellations | Lab 5 |
| 5 · Payment and Shipping | Payment methods, fraud checks, taxes, shipping strategy, dropshipping | Labs 6–7 |
| 6 · Marketing and Performance | Marketing plan, SEO, discounts, analytics, pixels, newsletter | Labs 8–9 |
