# Bloom & Brew — Store Policies

*Synthetic policy document for training use only. Bloom & Brew is a fictional retailer
created for the course AI for Retail (C398). Effective 1 July 2026, version 3.*

Bloom & Brew is a specialty home, gift and coffee retailer with six outlets — Orchard,
Tampines, Jurong East, Novena, Katong and Woodlands — and an online store.

---

## 1. Returns and Exchanges

**1.1** Unused items in their original packaging may be returned or exchanged **within 30
days of purchase** with proof of purchase (receipt or order confirmation email).

**1.2** Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 working days of the
item being received back in store or at the warehouse.

**1.3** Exchanges for a different colour, size or variant of the same product are made at
the original purchase price. If the replacement costs more, the difference is payable.

**1.4** Items returned **after 30 days** are not accepted for refund or exchange, except
where the item is faulty (see section 2).

**1.5** The following are **not returnable** unless faulty: coffee beans, tea and any
consumable that has been opened; gift cards; personalised or engraved items; and items
marked "final sale".

**1.6** Sale and clearance items may be exchanged within 14 days but are not refundable.

## 2. Faulty Goods

**2.1** Items found to be faulty are replaced or refunded in full within **12 months** of
purchase, regardless of the 30-day window in section 1.

**2.2** Glassware and ceramics that arrive broken must be reported **within 48 hours** of
delivery, with a photograph, to hello@bloomandbrew.example.

**2.3** Damage caused by misuse, drops, thermal shock (for example pouring boiling water
into a cold glass tumbler) or dishwasher use on hand-wash-only items is not covered.

## 3. Delivery

**3.1** Standard delivery within Singapore is **SGD 6.00**, free on orders over **SGD 80.00**.

**3.2** Standard delivery takes **2–4 working days**. Orders placed after 3pm are
processed the next working day.

**3.3** Click & Collect is free and ready at the chosen outlet within **4 hours** on
weekdays for items in stock at that outlet.

**3.4** We do not currently ship outside Singapore.

**3.5** If an order has not arrived within 7 working days, contact us and we will trace it
and, where it cannot be found, resend or refund.

## 4. Loyalty Programme — Bloom Club

**4.1** Membership is free. Tiers are **Bronze** (default), **Silver** (SGD 500 spend in a
rolling 12 months) and **Gold** (SGD 1,200 spend in a rolling 12 months).

**4.2** Members earn 1 point per SGD 1 spent. 100 points = SGD 5 off a future purchase.

**4.3** Silver members receive early access to seasonal ranges; Gold members additionally
receive free standard delivery on every order.

**4.4** Points expire 18 months after they are earned. Tier status is reviewed each
January.

## 5. Gift Cards

**5.1** Gift cards are valid for **12 months** from the date of purchase, in store and
online.

**5.2** Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash and are not refundable.

## 6. Product Care and Safety

**6.1** Candles: trim the wick to 5mm before each use, never burn for more than 4 hours at
a time, and never leave a burning candle unattended.

**6.2** Hand-wash-only items (travel flasks, pour-over sets, oak boards, glass vases) will
not be replaced if damaged in a dishwasher.

**6.3** Coffee is roasted to order and is best used within 4 weeks of the roast date
printed on the bag.

## 7. Customer Data and Privacy

**7.1** We collect only the customer data needed to fulfil an order and to run the Bloom
Club: name, contact details, delivery address and purchase history.

**7.2** Customer data is never sold to third parties.

**7.3** Marketing email is sent only to customers who have opted in, and every message
carries an unsubscribe link.

**7.4** A customer may request a copy of their data, or its deletion, by emailing
hello@bloomandbrew.example. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

**7.5** Customer data must not be entered into any external or public AI tool unless it has
first been anonymised in line with the store's AI usage policy.
