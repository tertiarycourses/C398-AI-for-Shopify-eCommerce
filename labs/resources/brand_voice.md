# Bloom & Brew — Brand Voice Guide

*Synthetic brand guide for training use only, for the course AI for Retail (C398).*

## Who we are

Bloom & Brew sells everyday things people use slowly and keep for years: stoneware mugs,
soy candles, single-origin coffee, linen and oak. Six outlets and an online store in
Singapore. Our customer is buying a small upgrade to an ordinary routine — the morning
coffee, the evening at home, a gift for someone who has enough things already.

## How we sound

**Warm, plain and specific.** We describe what a thing is made of, how big it is and how
to look after it. We trust the customer to decide whether they want it.

| We do | We don't |
|---|---|
| "350ml, double-walled, keeps a flat white hot for about 40 minutes." | "The ULTIMATE mug experience!" |
| "Hand-thrown in Portugal by a family workshop we have used since 2019." | "Artisanal luxury crafted by master artisans." |
| "Hand wash only — the matte glaze dulls in a dishwasher." | "Care instructions apply." |
| "Free delivery over SGD 80." | "AMAZING free delivery offer!!!" |

## Rules

1. **British English.** Colour, organise, litre, 40mm.
2. **No superlatives.** No "best", "ultimate", "perfect", "revolutionary", "must-have".
3. **No exclamation marks** in product copy. One is permitted per email, at most.
4. **Specific over evocative.** A measurement beats an adjective. If a sentence would
   still be true of a competitor's product, rewrite it.
5. **No invented provenance.** Only state a material, origin, certification or process
   that appears in the product data.
6. **Second person, present tense.** "You get two towels in the set", not "The customer
   receives two towels."
7. **Sentences under 20 words** where possible. Short paragraphs. No walls of text.
8. **Prices as SGD 32.00**, never $32 or 32 dollars.
9. **Never imply urgency we have not created.** No "selling fast" unless it is.
10. **Sustainability claims are only made where certified** — say "FSC certified oak",
    not "eco-friendly wood".

## Tone by channel

- **Product pages** — informative first. Facts, then one line on why it is nice to own.
- **Email** — a friendly note from a shop, not a broadcast. One idea, one call to action.
- **Social** — lighter, still specific. A question is fine. Two hashtags maximum.
- **Customer service** — direct, unhurried, and never defensive. Say what we will do and
  by when. If we got it wrong, say so in the first sentence.
- **In store signage** — six words or fewer, no punctuation gymnastics.

## Words we use

morning, evening, everyday, slowly, keep, made, weight, glaze, roast, linen, oak, refill.

## Words we avoid

curated, bespoke, elevate, indulge, pamper, game-changing, unlock, journey, solution,
"treat yourself", "you deserve".
