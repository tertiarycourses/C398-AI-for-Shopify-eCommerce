# Bloom & Brew — Frequently Asked Questions

*Synthetic FAQ for training use only, for the course AI for Retail (C398).
Answers here are consistent with store_policies.md — where the two differ, the policy wins.*

---

**Q1. How long do I have to return something?**
30 days from purchase, unused and in its original packaging, with your receipt or order
confirmation email. Faulty items are covered for 12 months.

**Q2. Can I exchange an item for a different colour?**
Yes, within 30 days, at the original purchase price. If the replacement costs more, the
difference is payable.

**Q3. Can I return coffee or tea?**
Only if it is unopened, or if it is faulty. Opened consumables cannot be returned.

**Q4. My order arrived broken. What do I do?**
Email hello@bloomandbrew.example within 48 hours of delivery with a photograph. We replace
or refund glassware and ceramics damaged in transit.

**Q5. How much is delivery?**
SGD 6.00 within Singapore, free on orders over SGD 80.00.

**Q6. How long does delivery take?**
2–4 working days. Orders placed after 3pm are processed the next working day.

**Q7. Do you deliver outside Singapore?**
Not at present. We ship within Singapore only.

**Q8. Can I collect from a store instead?**
Yes. Click & Collect is free and ready within 4 hours on weekdays, for items in stock at
your chosen outlet.

**Q9. My order has not arrived. What now?**
If it has been more than 7 working days, contact us. We will trace the order and either
resend it or refund you.

**Q10. How do I join the loyalty programme?**
Bloom Club membership is free — sign up in any outlet or at checkout online.

**Q11. How do Bloom Club points work?**
1 point per SGD 1 spent. 100 points is SGD 5 off a future purchase. Points expire 18 months
after they are earned.

**Q12. How do I reach Silver or Gold tier?**
Silver at SGD 500 spend in a rolling 12 months, Gold at SGD 1,200. Tier status is reviewed
each January.

**Q13. What do Gold members get?**
Early access to seasonal ranges, plus free standard delivery on every order.

**Q14. Do gift cards expire?**
Yes, 12 months from purchase. They cannot be exchanged for cash and are not refundable.

**Q15. Are your candles safe to leave burning?**
Never leave a burning candle unattended. Trim the wick to 5mm before each use and burn for
no more than 4 hours at a time.

**Q16. Can I put my mug in the dishwasher?**
Our stoneware cups and mugs are dishwasher safe. Travel flasks, pour-over sets, oak boards
and glass vases are hand wash only — check the product page for the item you have.

**Q17. How fresh is your coffee?**
It is roasted to order. Use it within 4 weeks of the roast date printed on the bag.

**Q18. Do you offer gift wrapping?**
Gift boxes come ready to give. We do not currently offer separate gift wrapping.

**Q19. What do you do with my personal data?**
We use it to fulfil your order and run Bloom Club. We never sell it. You can request a copy
or deletion by emailing hello@bloomandbrew.example.

**Q20. How do I stop marketing emails?**
Use the unsubscribe link in any message, or email us and we will remove you.
